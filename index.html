What I’m doing with Auré organic now, and what I want to test after launch.

Right now, 5 to 10 slideshows are published daily across Maria and Official. They generate inexpensive reach and show which relationship language earns attention. After launch, those learnings can become openings for product-led video, with one clear Auré action showing viewers what the product can do.

What the current results show

Specific relationship language is earning attention.

The strongest current posts are personal and concrete: rules after betrayal, rebuilding a sense of safety, and behaviors that make recovery harder. Slideshows keep Auré in front of new people while showing which wounds, questions, and story structures are worth developing further.

Inexpensive reach

Fast creative learning

Hooks and emotional angles can be tested in volume without committing to expensive production.

A clear limitation

Slideshows can validate attention, but they cannot show Auré’s value as clearly as product-led video.

Format and product inspiration

A small library of references worth returning to.

These references are useful for different reasons. Some show how to open, some show how a product enters, and some show the tone that already resonates around betrayal and recovery. None of them should be copied wholesale.

What I want to test after launch

Connect strong openings to something viewers can understand in the product.

The format should follow the feature, not the other way around. Each product-led video needs one recognizable relationship moment, one real Auré action, and one result that is clear on screen.

First planned test

Wall of Text → Demo

Why first: It is closest to the emotional language already earning attention.

What is new: The paragraph creates the need; one Auré interaction has to complete the thought.

Boundary: This is an experimental hybrid until the product transition is tested.

When the action fits

POV → Demo

Opening: Show one private behavior instead of explaining the entire story.

Transition: Auré must be used naturally inside that same moment.

Boundary: A distress clip followed by an unrelated screen recording does not count.

Feature-dependent

Reaction or Feature-first

Reaction: Use only when Auré produces a result worth reacting to.

Feature-first: Lead with the product when one action creates an immediately legible output.

Boundary: Do not manufacture surprise or force a feature tour.

Every demo should make one sequence clear.

01

Situation or input

The viewer understands the relationship moment or what the creator gives Auré.

02

One Auré action

The creator completes one interaction rather than touring several screens.

03

Visible result

The output provides clarity, recognition, relief, or a useful next step without promising a fixed outcome.

What needs to be clear about the product

A useful feature is easier to market when its value is visible quickly.

This is not a request for a gimmicky or “viral” feature. It is a way to identify which real Auré flow can carry the strongest short-form demonstrations.

1Fast comprehension

A muted viewer can understand what changed without reading a long explanation.

2Clear sequence

One situation leads to one input, one interaction, and one visible result.

3Personal relevance

The result feels connected to a recognizable relationship experience rather than a generic feature.

4Emotional payoff

The output creates clarity, recognition, relief, or a useful next step.

5Repeatability

The same flow can support multiple hooks and situations without repeating one exact script.

6Safe promise

The demonstration does not claim to repair trust, resolve abuse, replace professional care, or guarantee recovery.

Final scripts still depend on the confirmed launch flow, visible output, stable build, privacy boundaries, approved screens, and approved product claims.

How the next decisions get made

Results decide what gets another round.

Views show that an opening can earn attention. Product-led tests should show whether that attention creates stronger intent. Organic attribution will remain directional, so confidence comes from repeated patterns rather than one spike.

Attention winner

Earns attention repeatedly

A hook or concept beats the relevant account baseline more than once through views, engagement, shares, or profile activity.

Product winner

Creates measurable intent

The video appears alongside stronger link activity, installs, or the first meaningful product action once that event is defined and available.

Scale candidate

Survives wider distribution

A repeat organic winner becomes a sensible Spark Ad or paid Instagram test. Paid performance determines whether it can scale predictably.

The working loop

1 · Test recognition

Keep the daily slideshow layer running across Auré’s clearest relationship pains.

2 · Find repeat winners

Separate hooks that repeatedly outperform the account baseline from isolated spikes.

3 · Match hook to feature

Choose the Auré interaction that genuinely addresses the situation created by the opening.

4 · Rebuild as video

Use wall-of-text, POV, reaction, or feature-first execution only when the product supports it naturally.

5 · Compare attention and action

Organize posts by account, hook, format, time, views, and engagement, then compare spikes with profile activity, links, installs, and available product signals.

6 · Scale combinations

Create variations around hook–feature pairings that perform repeatedly and test strong organic videos through Auré’s existing paid operation.

Profile activity and tracked links provide supporting evidence, but no single organic post should be credited with every install. Repeated correlation is the practical standard.

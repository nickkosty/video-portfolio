Use slideshows to find the hooks. Use Auré videos to turn those hooks into product interest.

I’m publishing every day to learn which relationship wounds, questions, and phrases earn attention. After launch, I’ll move the strongest hooks into videos that show one real Auré action and one clear payoff.

Slideshows test the opening. The product demonstration has to complete it.

What changes after launch

The video creates the feeling. Auré provides the next step.

Every format follows the same arc: recognizable relationship moment, one real product action, one legible payoff. The promise is not “Auré fixes the relationship.” It is “I finally have somewhere private to begin processing this.”

POV + Demo

The message I couldn’t send

“POV: you’ve typed the same message ten times and still can’t explain how you feel.”

Show: Type, delete, retype, close Messages.

Demo: Enter the uncensored feeling into Auré.

Payoff: One short reflection or clarifying question. Do not make Auré write the perfect reply.

Reaction + Demo

The “how did I not know this existed?” reveal

“All this time trying to explain how I felt and now there’s an app for this?”

Show: Quiet frustration followed by surprised recognition.

Demo: Hard cut into one real Auré action.

Payoff: A clear output, question, or support route. Recognition, not a fake instant cure.

Wall of Text + Demo

The thought before somebody needs Auré

“I kept rewriting the message because every version sounded angry, dramatic, or impossible to explain.”

Show: Quiet creator footage under one readable emotional setup.

Demo: Cut after one complete read into the matching Auré flow.

Payoff: The shortest useful part of the real response.

What must be confirmed in Auré

The creative depends on one product flow viewers can understand immediately.

1Primary launch action

Which real Auré interaction should the first videos demonstrate?

2Input and output

What does the person give Auré, what appears next, and how quickly does the useful payoff arrive?

3Visual clarity

Can the output be understood in a few seconds without reading an entire private conversation?

4Confirmed support surfaces

Which private conversation, peer support, circles, grounding, reflection, clarifying-question, or routing experiences genuinely ship?

5Privacy and claims

What can be shown safely, and what wording is approved without implying clinical certainty or guaranteed outcomes?

6Demo materials

I need a stable build, seeded examples, approved recordings, and real flows so the content is based on the product rather than guesses.

How we judge and scale it

Views find the hook. Product actions decide whether the format scales.

Attention

Views and watch behavior

Did the hook stop people?

Intent

Profile visits

Did they want more context?

Traffic

Link clicks

Did the video create action?

Acquisition

Installs

Did interest reach the product?

Value

First meaningful action

Did the user actually engage?

Find one winner first

Identify one repeatable combination of hook, video container, and Auré flow before expanding production.

Then add creators

Once the format converts consistently, reproduce it through more creator perspectives and story-led accounts.

Later-stage scale

Automation, clippers, and 50 to 100 daily slideshow posts are options only after the economics and conversion path are proven.

Paid and organic

Paid gives controlled acquisition. Organic discovers the language and formats that can break out and potentially feed the paid system.

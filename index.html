Auré organic update

The current Auré organic system and what comes after launch.

Right now, 5 to 10 slideshows are published daily across Maria and Official. They provide inexpensive reach, consistent output, and fast hook testing. After launch, the slideshows keep running as a supporting top-of-funnel layer while UGC and app-demo videos do more of the conversion work.

What the current tests show

Slideshows generate inexpensive reach and surface concepts worth testing as video.

Slideshows support the broader organic strategy through inexpensive top-of-funnel reach, easy production, and fast hook testing. By themselves, they are unlikely to drive the strongest conversion results. Their larger value is keeping attention flowing while revealing which hooks and concepts should be rebuilt as UGC and product-led video.

Top-of-funnel views

They keep Auré in front of new people through easy, feed-native content.

Hook testing

Easy volume

The format is cheap, repeatable, and can eventually be scheduled or distributed with less menial work.

Lower conversion

The limitation is that slideshows cannot demonstrate Auré as clearly as UGC and app-centered video.

What changes after launch

Post-launch videos connect a recognizable situation to one confirmed Auré action.

Three initial containers will be tested: POV plus demo, reaction plus demo, and text-led story plus demo. Scripts will be finalized only after the launch flow, visible output, privacy limits, and approved claims are confirmed.

POV + Demo

The message I couldn’t send

“POV: you’ve typed the same message ten times and still can’t explain how you feel.”

Show: Type, delete, retype, close Messages.

Demo: Cut into one confirmed Auré flow and show the input, interaction, and visible output.

Payoff: State only the approved benefit the demonstrated flow actually delivers.

Reaction + Demo

The “how did I not know this existed?” reveal

“All this time trying to explain how I felt and now there’s an app for this?”

Show: Quiet frustration followed by surprised recognition.

Demo: Hard cut into one confirmed Auré action; do not tour the whole app.

Payoff: Make the approved result clear with sound off.

Wall of Text + Demo

The thought before somebody needs Auré

“I kept rewriting the message because every version sounded angry, dramatic, or impossible to explain.”

Show: Quiet creator footage under one readable emotional setup.

Demo: Cut after one complete read into one confirmed Auré flow.

Payoff: End on the real visible output rather than a broad promise.

The first post-launch test

Keep one confirmed product flow constant while testing the three video containers with two or three proven slideshow hooks. Publish multiple versions under comparable conditions so the hook and format can be compared fairly.

What needs to be ready

Product-led content starts once there is something clear and visual to show.

1Feature to demonstrate

Choose the launch feature or flow that is easiest to understand and most compelling to watch.

2Visible payoff

Make the input, interaction, and result clear enough to understand within a few seconds.

3Working demo

Provide a stable build, seeded examples, and approved screen recordings so the content shows the real product rather than guesses.

4Privacy and claims

Confirm what can be shown safely and which benefits can be described without promising guaranteed outcomes.

How performance is judged and scaled

Organic attribution is directional, not exact.

Content performance and install trends should be visible in one place. Each post can be organized by account, hook, format, publish time, views, and engagement, then compared with changes in installs. Links provide extra evidence, but the main signal is whether content spikes repeatedly correlate with install spikes.

System

Performance tracking

The operating loop

Maintain consistent output, use performance data to identify repeat winners, create more variations, compare content spikes with install movement, test the strongest videos as paid creative, and feed the results back into the next round of content.

Slideshows

Keep daily slideshow publishing in place for inexpensive reach and hook testing. Schedule posts and reduce repetitive distribution work where useful.

Product-led video

Test different creators, hooks, and formats after launch, then compare the results with profile activity, link clicks, and install trends.

What to double down on

Prioritize hooks and formats that perform repeatedly and appear alongside stronger product interest. High views alone are not enough.

Organic into paid

High-performing organic videos are already validated as attention-grabbing creative and often translate well into paid ads. Test those winners as TikTok Spark Ads or paid Instagram creative and scale the ones that continue performing.

Review and scale

Use the available views, engagement, profile activity, clicks, and install trends to decide what deserves more variations and distribution.

Before launchWhat I’m focused on

For now, I want to get really good at slideshows.

They are easy to make at volume, they help grow the accounts, and they let me learn which ideas are worth carrying into videos later.

Content

Make four versions of ideas we already like

Change one thing at a time, such as the hook, topic, images, or final slide.

Still try one new thing

Leave room for new ideas without making every post completely random.

Accounts

Maria is more personal and relatable

More conversational content, with first-person stories only when they are real and supported.

Official is clearer about Auré

Helpful reader-facing content that can explain or show the product without pretending to have personal experiences.

Learning

Look for patterns across several posts

Do not decide an idea is amazing or dead because of one post.

Keep the flops too

A weak post can still show me which hook, topic, or account was the wrong fit.

What we still do not know

Views do not tell us which posts will get downloads or turn into active users. We can only learn that properly after launch.

After launchWhat I want to try

Once the app is live, I want to make videos that actually show it.

Slideshows can still help with reach, but videos let someone see what Auré does. These are the first formats I think are worth trying. We will only know what works after we post them.

Starting point

Simple app demo

Start with a real situation, then show the useful part of Auré clearly.

Why start hereIt gives us the clearest idea of whether showing the app makes people curious enough to click or download.
Idea 01

Emotional POV → app demo

“POV: you found out he cheated and you are spiraling at 3 AM,” followed by what using Auré could look like in that moment.

Watch out forIt needs to be clearly framed as a scenario, not a fake real-life story or testimonial.
Idea 02

Text over girl b-roll → app demo

A relatable thought in white or yellow text over emotional b-roll, then a cut into the app.

What I’d testThe text style, how long the setup lasts, and when the app appears.
Idea 03

Reaction + app walkthrough

React to a relationship scenario or common question, then show how Auré could help in that moment.

Why it might workThe reaction gets attention, then the walkthrough shows something real instead of just saying “download the app.”
Idea 04

Creator videos

A creator talks through a real problem, tries Auré, or shows how they would use it in a normal TikTok style.

ImportantNo fake testimonials or made-up recovery stories.
Small test

AI podcast clips

Try one or two clips because the format can get views, but do not make it the main plan.

Open questionWhether people trust it and care about Auré, or whether it only gets empty views.

How I’d test all of this

I’d post small groups where I only change one big thing, like the hook, when the app appears, or which feature gets shown. Before launch I’d look at views, watch time, shares, and saves. After launch I’d also look at clicks, downloads, and whether people actually use the app.

A few questionsStuff I’m unsure about

These are the main things I want your opinion on.

I do not need you to review every post. These are just the areas where I do not want to guess what feels right for Auré.

1

Relationship advice

My default would be to avoid pushing people to stay, leave, forgive, or reconcile, and never act like every relationship follows the same stages.

What do you think?How far should we go with formats like “restart with the same person,” relationship stages, and general advice?
2

Therapy wording

My default would be to call Auré support and education, not therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or a replacement for professional help.

What do you think?Is there specific wording you want me and future creators to use?
3

Dramatized POV videos

My default would be to use fictional scenarios only when they are clearly framed as POV, not as real testimonials.

What do you think?Are those kinds of emotional scenarios okay for the brand?
4

What we can show at launch

My default would be to show only real features and send people toward one clear next step.

What do you think?Which parts of the app should I build content around, and what should we ask viewers to do?

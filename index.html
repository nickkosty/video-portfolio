Auré organic update

What I’m doing with Auré organic now, and what I want to test after launch.

Right now I’m posting 5 to 8 slideshows a day across Maria and Official. I’m using them for inexpensive reach, consistent output, and hook testing. Once the app is live, I want to keep the slideshows running while I test UGC and app-demo videos that can convert more strongly.

What the tests are teaching me

Slideshows bring easy traffic and show me what to turn into video.

Slideshows are still useful content on their own. They are inexpensive top-of-funnel traffic, easy to mass produce, and a fast way for me to test emotional hooks. I do not expect them to convert as well as UGC or product demos, so I use the attention and hook data to decide what I should rebuild as video.

Top-of-funnel views

They keep Auré in front of new people through easy, feed-native content.

Hook testing

I can test many wounds, questions, and phrases without expensive production.

Easy volume

The format is cheap, repeatable, and can eventually be scheduled or distributed with less menial work.

Lower conversion

The limitation is that slideshows cannot demonstrate Auré as clearly as UGC and app-centered video.

What changes after launch

The video creates the feeling. Auré provides the next step.

Every format follows the same arc: recognizable relationship moment, one real product action, one legible payoff. The promise is not “Auré fixes the relationship.” It is “I finally have somewhere private to begin processing this.”

POV + Demo

The message I couldn’t send

“POV: you’ve typed the same message ten times and still can’t explain how you feel.”

Show: Type, delete, retype, close Messages.

Demo: Enter the uncensored feeling into Auré.

Payoff: One short reflection or clarifying question. Do not make Auré write the perfect reply.

Reaction + Demo

The “how did I not know this existed?” reveal

“All this time trying to explain how I felt and now there’s an app for this?”

Show: Quiet frustration followed by surprised recognition.

Demo: Hard cut into one real Auré action.

Payoff: A clear output, question, or support route. Recognition, not a fake instant cure.

Wall of Text + Demo

The thought before somebody needs Auré

“I kept rewriting the message because every version sounded angry, dramatic, or impossible to explain.”

Show: Quiet creator footage under one readable emotional setup.

Demo: Cut after one complete read into the matching Auré flow.

Payoff: The shortest useful part of the real response.

The first post-launch test

I will choose one confirmed Auré flow and test it through these three video containers using two or three hooks already supported by slideshow performance. That lets me see whether the hook, format, or product payoff is driving the result.

What must be confirmed in Auré

The creative depends on one product flow viewers can understand immediately.

1Exact ICP

I need to pinpoint who Auré is for, what relationship moment they are in, what they already believe, and the language they use. I will use the Mark Builds Brands guides to sharpen this before finalizing the copy and creator briefs.

2Primary launch action

Which real Auré interaction should the first videos demonstrate?

3Input and output

What does the person give Auré, what appears next, and how quickly does the useful payoff arrive?

4Visual clarity

Can the output be understood in a few seconds without reading an entire private conversation?

5Confirmed support surfaces

Which private conversation, peer support, circles, grounding, reflection, clarifying-question, or routing experiences genuinely ship?

6Privacy and claims

What can be shown safely, and what wording is approved without implying clinical certainty or guaranteed outcomes?

7Demo materials

I need a stable build, seeded examples, approved recordings, and real flows so the content is based on the product rather than guesses.

Views find the hook. Product actions decide whether the format scales.

05

Feed the learning back

Conversion data tells me which hooks, formats, creators, and product flows deserve more volume.

Slideshows

I keep them pumping for inexpensive top-of-funnel views and hook data. I can schedule posts and reduce repetitive distribution work without pretending the creative itself is automatic.

UGC and app video

I test many creators, hooks, and containers because this is the layer I expect to convert more strongly than slideshows.

Organic into paid

If a video works organically, I test it as a Spark Ad or paid Instagram creative. Organic validation improves the starting signal, but paid conversion still has to be proven separately.

Tracking

I need every account, hook, format, and creator tied to a unique link or attribution path so I can connect views to clicks, installs, and the first meaningful in-product action.

Later-stage scale

Once I find a repeatable high-converting combination, I double down through more creators, more story-led accounts, and eventually clippers or higher-volume slideshow distribution.

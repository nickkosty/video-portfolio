Auré organic content

How I’m testing hooks now, then turning the winners into app videos.

Right now I’m posting 5 to 8 slideshows every day across Maria and the official Auré account. The slideshows keep content moving, but their bigger value is showing me which emotional hooks people actually respond to.

The plan is simple: use slideshows to find the language that earns attention, then carry those winning hooks into POV, reaction, and wall-of-text videos that show one clear Auré action after launch.

What I want to test after launch

The hook earns attention. The demo gives it a payoff.

The exact hook can change across betrayal, breakups, uncertainty, loneliness, grief, or difficulty expressing a feeling. The repeatable format stays the same: establish the moment first, then show one Auré interaction that makes sense inside that moment.

The feature question

A marketable feature has to make sense visually in a few seconds.

“Inherently viral” should not mean publishing somebody’s private pain. It should mean the interaction is easy to demonstrate, the result feels personal, and viewers immediately want to try the same process for themselves.

01

Is the output instantly legible?

A viewer should understand what Auré returned without reading an entire conversation.

02

Does it complete the opening tension?

The output should organize, reveal, or clarify the feeling introduced by the hook.

03

Can it be shown privately?

The demo cannot depend on exposing sensitive user data or publishing deeply personal information.

04

Would viewers want their own result?

The desire should be to try the interaction, not merely watch someone else’s outcome.

Possible visual payoffs, only if they genuinely exist at launch

  • A concise reflection that organizes the user’s feeling
  • One clarifying question that changes how the situation is understood
  • A grounding interaction with a visually clear progression
  • A recommendation for the right kind of support to use next
  • A transition from a private conversation into appropriate peer support

What needs to be clear about Auré

The final video concepts depend on what the launch build can really show.

Launch features

Which three product actions will actually be ready, and which one looks strongest on camera?

Input and output

What does the user give Auré, what does Auré return, and can that transformation be understood quickly?

Peer support

Can a video truthfully show a transition into peers or circles, and what does that experience look like without exposing private information?

Demo material

When can I work from a demo build, real screenshots, and confirmed flows instead of guessing at the product?

Channel roles

Paid remains the more controllable and predictable traffic source. Organic keeps generating creative reps, new hooks, and occasional breakout reach.

The goal is not to make Auré look like it magically fixes a relationship. The goal is to show that when a relationship experience becomes too much to carry alone, Auré gives the person somewhere private to begin processing it and a clearer next step.

The strategy in one sentence

Build the learning system before the product asks for conversion.

The current work is not just filling a content calendar. It is reducing the creative uncertainty Auré would otherwise face at launch.

01 / Reach

Earn attention with formats people already understand.

I study proven relationship slideshow structures and preserve the hook, progression, payoff, density, and swipe mechanism instead of forcing every source into a generic brand template.

02 / Trust

Translate attention into an accurate Auré perspective.

Each draft is routed to the account that can tell the story truthfully. The content remains useful first, with Auré introduced only where the product naturally supports the relationship experience.

03 / Learning

Keep a traceable line from source to post to result.

ContentOS preserves the source, draft, image sequence, status, public URL, and performance history so we can learn from controlled tests rather than random posting.

Context and judgment

The assistant is the control layer.

  • Understands Michael's feedback and current brand boundaries
  • Preserves the mechanism of strong inspiration
  • Infers the correct account and creative mode
  • Flags sensitive concepts before they become brand statements
  • Catches semantic issues that a form cannot understand

Durability and execution

ContentOS is the system of record.

  • Stores source and draft lineage
  • Supports visual review, image swaps, rendering, and export
  • Tracks statuses, live URLs, and public performance
  • Reconciles confident caption matches when the dashboard refreshes
  • Creates the data history needed for later learning

Current reality: publishing remains manual, recurring generation is paused while the workflow is being tuned, and public-post reconciliation currently depends on a dashboard refresh. Full automatic routing, explicit creative-mode fields, and hourly read-only reconciliation are planned improvements, not features I am claiming are already complete.

Preserve the control.

For source recreation, keep the hook mechanism, slide count, reveal order, emotional progression, point order, approximate density, and image sequence whenever possible.

Change one variable at a time.

A winner variation should name the variable being tested, such as hook wording or aspect ratio, while the body structure and payoff remain fixed.

Do not lead with an ad.

The reviewed winners earn attention through a relationship problem first. Auré belongs at the exact support moment, not in a generic opening pitch.

High views do not override positioning.

Formats that pressure reconciliation, minimize harmful conflict, require forgiveness, make clinical claims, or materially broaden Auré's position still need review.

What can run now

Separate the proven mechanics from the sensitive message.

The current v3 Auré positioning supports a broad range of difficult relationship experiences. The main approval question is not whether a topic has views. It is whether the message is safe, truthful, and aligned.

Fits current positioning

Communication micro-rules

Specific language, check-ins, repair attempts, listening, and emotionally clear communication.

General healthy relationship habits

Mutual respect, honest reflection, boundaries, and practices that do not instruct someone to remain in a relationship.

Carefully framed breakup coping

Content supporting people through separation or emotional aftermath without diagnosing or promising outcomes.

Broader relationship changes

Infidelity, separation, divorce, partner loss, unhealthy relationships, loneliness, and difficult change, consistent with the new homepage positioning.

Needs Michael's decision

Reconciliation-first formats

Content that assumes staying together, restarting, or repairing is the preferred outcome.

Breakup is failure framing

Hooks such as “your relationship needs a reset, not a breakup” can pressure people to remain.

Conflict normalization

“Normal fights” content can become unsafe when it blurs the line between ordinary conflict and harmful behavior.

Therapy, mental-health, or universal-stage claims

These can imply clinical authority, oversimplify experiences, or establish new positioning.

How this helps before launch

Pre-launch content reduces the cold-start problem. Launch turns the same engine toward conversion.

The creative system stays. The job of the content, the call to action, and the evidence used to select winners become more product-specific.

Now / pre-launch

Build reach, recognition, trust, and a library of proven controls.

The immediate goal is to learn what earns attention and what language people respond to before every post has to carry an install target.

  • Test repeatable slideshow mechanics at volume
  • Warm the Official account and clarify what Auré supports
  • Build audience familiarity across the broader relationship-change position
  • Collect comments and audience language for future creative
  • Prepare a launch-ready library of hooks, formats, and objections
  • Route appropriate interest toward the waitlist or early access
Launch and after

Connect winning attention to real product use and measurable acquisition.

Organic content becomes both top-of-funnel discovery and a direct demonstration of how Auré supports difficult moments.

  • Shift calls to action from coming soon to try Auré or download
  • Create in-the-moment AI Companion demonstrations
  • Show personalized practices, peer connection, matched groups, and educational content accurately
  • Build founder-led and behind-the-product stories where supported
  • Use real user stories and reactions only with consent and truthful framing
  • Adapt winning relationship formats while integrating the exact relevant Auré support path

The post-launch learning loop

Public views find opportunities. Owned behavior decides what actually scales.

Once attribution is available, a format should not be called a winner only because it receives views.

01Source mechanism and hypothesis
02Watch time and completion
03Shares, saves, comments, and profile activity
04Bio click, store visit, or landing-page action
05Install, signup, or account creation where measurable
06Activation, retention, and meaningful product behavior

The long-term goal is controlled iteration: keep the winning mechanism, change one named variable, connect each live post back to its draft, and let Auré's own downstream behavior outrank competitor view counts.
